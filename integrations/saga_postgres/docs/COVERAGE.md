# Saga PostgreSQL design coverage

- One nested PostgreSQL storage context owns the database projection of Saga's port. Parent Saga remains the authority for workflow transitions, checkpoint meaning, and recovery permission.
- `captured` describes design coverage rather than tested deployment completeness. Intended extensions and missing evidence remain explicit in the layer's pending ledger.

- The callable inventory contains 14 surfaces: six public adapter functions, seven Storage callbacks, and one renewal callback. The behavior areas cover configuration, migration, admission/reads, ownership, conditional progress, cancellation, and discovery.
- Delivery scheduling, asynchronous UI, provider policy, application business entities, and domain-event publication are inapplicable to this adapter. Multi-VM, restart/failover, media, and total-deadline claims remain unresolved rather than declared tested.
